About Us

Strategy-led learning, inspired by Chanakya.

Named after one of India's greatest teachers and strategists, Urban Chanakya makes future-ready skills accessible to every learner, from a school student discovering AI for the first time to a teacher bringing it into the classroom.

We combine structured curricula, real-world projects and globally used platforms to turn curiosity into capability, and capability into careers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do learners need a technical background?

No. Our foundation programs start from the basics and are designed for complete beginners, including school students.

Are programs online or offline?

Both. We run on-campus sessions at schools and colleges as well as live online cohorts.

Will participants receive a certificate?

Yes. Learners who complete a program and its project receive an Urban Chanakya certificate of completion.

How do I earn the Google AI Educator Series certificate?

Register on Google's GES page and select Urban Chanakya as your training partner. See the steps.

Can you customise a program for our institution?

Absolutely. We tailor duration, depth and tools to your learners' level and goals. Get in touch to discuss.
