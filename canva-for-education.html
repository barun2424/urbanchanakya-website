FAQ

Canva Education questions

Is Canva Education really free?

Yes. Canva has committed to keeping Canva Education completely free for K–12 (primary and secondary) teachers and their students, as well as qualifying schools and districts.

Who can get access?

K–12 teachers verify themselves with a school email address or proof of employment. Students get access through their teacher or school. Check Canva's eligibility guidelines for full details.

Does it work with our LMS?

Yes. Canva Education integrates with Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Schoology and D2L.

What does Urban Chanakya's training include?

We run practical workshops for teachers, students and school leaders, covering getting verified, designing lessons and assignments, collaborative projects, and responsible use of Canva's AI features. Programs can be tailored to your institution.
