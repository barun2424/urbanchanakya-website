What Our Trainers Deliver

The Google AI Educator Series

The Google AI Educator Series (GES) provides no-cost, comprehensive AI literacy training to educators across India. Our trainers deliver it to schools and higher-education institutions.

AI Educator Series
  • 01Build AI confidence among educators, aligned with NEP 2020
  • 02Learn to use Gemini with the PARTS prompting framework
  • 03Promote safe and responsible use of AI
  • 04Earn badges and the GES certificate

Safe & responsible AI

Equips educators to guide students to use AI tools safely, ethically and effectively.

Regional languages

Training in English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Assamese, Odia and Punjabi.

Domain identity

Institution domain-level identity for secure, seamless collaboration across campus.

Google Faculty Groups

A peer network of educators for continuous learning and shared best practices.

Signature Method

Prompting with PARTS

Educators learn to get the best responses from Gemini by building prompts in five parts. Tap each letter.

P

Persona

Identify who you are in relation to the task, so the AI can tailor its output to your role.

Example

"You are a Senior Faculty Developer and expert in Academic Assessment."
